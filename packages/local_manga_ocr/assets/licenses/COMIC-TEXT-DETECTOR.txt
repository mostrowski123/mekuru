Modified for Mekuru on 2026-09-10:
The detector preprocessing, DBNet contour extraction, YOLO suppression and
text-block/line grouping were ported to Kotlin/OpenCV. Runtime inference uses
ONNX Runtime. Resource bounds, input validation and lifecycle control were added.
For rectangular DBNet mini-boxes the offset is calculated analytically rather
than invoking Python's polygon-offset dependency.

Corresponding source includes the native plugin, its build files, model manifest,
Mekuru integration, and reproduction instructions in docs/development/local-ocr.md.
It accompanies Mekuru's source release at https://github.com/mostrowski123/mekuru.

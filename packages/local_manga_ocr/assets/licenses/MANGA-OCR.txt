Mekuru uses the FP16 encoder and INT8 decoder ONNX graphs of the export and
the character vocabulary of the source model. Image preprocessing, greedy
decoding and text post-processing were reimplemented in Kotlin to match the
manga_ocr Python package. Line alignment, cancellation, storage and
integration are Mekuru additions. Model weights are downloaded separately
from the pinned upstream repositories. No manga training images or
transcriptions are distributed.

The corresponding Mekuru integration source and build instructions accompany
the Mekuru source release: https://github.com/mostrowski123/mekuru

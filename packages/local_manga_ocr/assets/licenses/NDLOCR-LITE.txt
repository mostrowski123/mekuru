The files are not shipped with Mekuru: they are downloaded from the pinned
revision above when the user installs the optional text-book reader, and
verified by size and SHA-256. Mekuru uses the model to read the long text
lines of scanned books during on-device OCR. Line cropping, resizing,
normalisation and greedy decoding were reimplemented in Dart and Kotlin to
match NDLOCR-Lite's Python code; they are not part of the model.

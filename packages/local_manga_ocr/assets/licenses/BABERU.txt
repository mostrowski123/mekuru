Mekuru uses the FP16 vision and INT8 decoder ONNX graphs and character vocabulary.
The preprocessing and host decoding loop were adapted from onnx_infer.py for
Android/Kotlin on 2026-09-10. Line alignment, cancellation, storage and integration
are Mekuru additions. Model weights are downloaded separately from the pinned
upstream repository. No manga training images or transcriptions are distributed.

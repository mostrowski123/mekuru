Everything you need to keep turning pages

Manga you can tap

Open mokuro manga and tap words right away, for free. With Pro, on-device OCR reads the text of any page, offline.

Vertical novels

Read EPUB books in vertical or horizontal text, with furigana on all kanji, only above your JLPT level, or only on kanji you haven't learned on WaniKani.

Offline dictionaries

Install a full Japanese-English dictionary in one tap, add more languages, or import your own Yomitan dictionaries.

Smart lookups

Conjugated verbs and compound words lead to the right entry, with pitch accent and word frequency where your dictionaries have them.

Sentence translation

Stuck on a whole sentence? Translate it on your device, in the same sheet as the dictionary.

Free books

Start with over 17,000 Aozora Bunko classics and free graded readers, sorted by estimated level.

Vocabulary and Anki

Save words with the sentence you found them in. Send cards to AnkiDroid, AnkiMobile or AnkiConnect, or export CSV.

Your library

Import EPUB, PDF and CBZ files, sort them into collections, sync with your own Komga or Kavita server, and back everything up.

Free and open source

The reader, dictionaries and Anki export are free, with no ads and no account. The code is AGPL-3.0, with an optional one-time Pro upgrade.

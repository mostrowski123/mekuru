# Installing Mekuru

Install Mekuru from Google Play on Android, or from the App Store on iPhone and iPad.

## On Android

### Google Play

1. Open [Mekuru on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=moe.matthew.mekuru).
2. Tap **Install**.

### Beta testing

Beta testers get new versions of Mekuru before everyone else.

1. Join the [mekuru-testing-group](https://groups.google.com/g/mekuru-testing-group) Google Group.
2. Open the [Google Play testing page](https://play.google.com/apps/testing/moe.matthew.mekuru) and join the test.
3. Install or update Mekuru from that page.

### APK from GitHub

You can also install Mekuru without Google Play.

1. Open the latest [GitHub release](https://github.com/mostrowski123/mekuru/releases).
2. Download `app-play-release.apk`.
3. Open the file on your phone. Android may ask you to allow installs from that app first.

Each release also has `app-parallel-release.apk`. It installs as a separate app called Mekuru Parallel, with its own library and settings. Use it to try a new version without touching your books.

To build Mekuru yourself, follow the [README on GitHub](https://github.com/mostrowski123/mekuru#getting-started).

## On iPhone and iPad

You need iOS 26 or iPadOS 26, or newer.

1. Open [Mekuru on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/mekuru-japanese-manga-reader/id6814013845).
2. Tap **Get**.

## First launch

Mekuru opens on the **Library** tab. Your library is empty and shows these buttons:

- **Import EPUB** and **Import Manga** add your own files. See [Importing Books](importing-books.md) and [Importing Manga](importing-manga.md).
- **Get Dictionaries** installs the starter pack. Do this first, so you can look up words. See [Setting Up Dictionaries](dictionaries.md).
- **Restore Backup** brings back your data from a Mekuru backup, for example one made on your old phone. See [Backup & Restore](../settings/backup-restore.md).
- **Browse free books** opens a catalog of free Japanese books. See [Free Books](free-books.md).

You don't need an account.

Mekuru uses your phone's language when it is English, Japanese, Spanish, Indonesian or Simplified Chinese, and English otherwise. To change it, open **You › Settings › App Language**.
